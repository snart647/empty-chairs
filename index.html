Why the board looks like this
Carousels carry it

29 carousels produced every one of the 990 followers won this quarter, plus 75 percent of all reach. Four of the five weekly posts are carousels for that reason alone.

One reel, for reach only

Reels reached 98 592 people from 12 posts, then sent zero to the profile. Keep one a week, judge it on views, never blame it for follower growth.

Thursday 15:00 is flagship

11 046 median reach on Thursday against 3 721 on Wednesday, and 15:00 is the strongest hour at 13 094. The week's best post goes there, every week.

Photos are service posts

Six photos, two saves, zero followers. They are quotes and testimonials doing a different job. Stop scoring them against the teaching content.

Last call, 0 to 14 days

Owns the whole week. Every course post points at this room. After the start date the seat is gone for good.

Active, 15 to 45 days

Gets the flagship carousel. This is where most bookings are actually made.

Build, 46 to 90 days

Gets the monthly theme, not the weekly one. Teach the subject, never mention the date yet.

Seed, 90 days and beyond

Authority content with nothing to sell, so the room feels obvious by the time it opens.

Two lanes, not one

Performance posts are judged on reach, saves and followers. Brand posts are judged on whether a stranger believes this is a real company with real rooms. One brand slot a week is the floor, and course recaps come on top of it.

Recaps are the only proof you film yourself

Nine of the twelve weeks contain a course day. The evening after, the room goes out as it looked: faces, movement, laughter. A single group photo counts, and beats a better edit that never ships.

Reviews come from your routine

The proof slot carries the frame, the checklist and the consent questions. The quote itself is pasted in, never written here, because a testimonial nobody said is worth less than no testimonial at all.

Losing on reach is allowed

A brand post that reaches 900 people and makes four of them trust you has done its job. Do not move it to Thursday and do not compare it to a carousel. Different lane, different scoreboard.
